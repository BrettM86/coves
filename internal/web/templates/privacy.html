Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Coves Team ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Coves mobile application and website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

Summary: Coves is built on the atProto protocol. Your data is stored on your Personal Data Server (PDS), which you control. We only index and cache publicly available data from the network to provide our service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in to Coves, we receive the following from your atProto identity:

  • Decentralized Identifier (DID): Your unique identifier on the atProto network
  • Handle: Your username (e.g., yourname.bsky.social)
  • Profile Information: Display name, bio, and avatar that you've set on your PDS

1.2 Content You Create

When you use Coves, the content you create is written to your PDS:

  • Posts and comments
  • Votes (likes and downvotes)
  • Community memberships

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

Data Type Collected?
Passwords No - OAuth only
Device identifiers (IMEI, serial numbers) No
Location data No
Contacts No
Photos/media from your device No
Analytics or telemetry No
Advertising identifiers No
Crash reports No

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Display your profile and content within the app
  • Show you posts, comments, and communities
  • Process your votes and interactions

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3. The atProto Protocol and Federation

Important: Coves is built on the atProto (AT Protocol), a federated social networking protocol. This affects how your data works.

3.1 Your Personal Data Server (PDS)

Your content (posts, comments, votes) is stored on your PDS, not on Coves servers. You may host your own PDS or use a hosted provider. You maintain control over your data at the PDS level.

3.2 What Coves Stores

Coves operates an AppView that indexes publicly available data from the atProto network firehose. This means:

  • We cache and index your public content to provide fast access
  • Your authentication tokens are stored encrypted on your device
  • We do not store copies of your private data

3.3 Federation

Because atProto is federated, your public content may be visible on other applications and services that use the protocol. This is a feature of the protocol, not something Coves controls. When you post content, consider that it may be indexed and displayed by other atProto services.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where Data is Stored

  • Your device: Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted storage (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences)
  • Your PDS: Your content is stored on your Personal Data Server
  • Our servers: Located in Canada, used for indexing public atProto data

4.2 Security Measures

  • OAuth 2.0 with DPoP for authentication
  • Encrypted token storage on device
  • HTTPS for all network communications
  • No plaintext storage of sensitive data

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: For content delivery, DDoS protection, and DNS. Cloudflare may process your IP address and request metadata. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1 Deleting Your Coves Data

You can request deletion of your Coves account at Delete Account. When your request is completed, we remove your profile, posts, comments, votes, subscriptions, and other associated account data from the active Coves AppView database. Signing out does not delete your account or content.

6.2 Database Backups

Copies of deleted data may remain in database backups for up to 30 days, until those backups expire. Backups are restricted to authorized personnel and used for disaster recovery, rather than serving content or restoring deleted accounts. If we restore a backup, we reapply completed deletion requests before returning the restored data to normal service.

6.3 Limited Records Retained After Deletion

We retain a limited deletion record containing your account identifier and the deletion date to prevent your deleted data from being imported into Coves again. Moderation and safety reports may also be retained where necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or meet legal obligations. These records are separate from your public profile and content. They are retained only for those purposes and for as long as necessary.

6.4 Your PDS and Other Services

Deleting your Coves AppView account does not delete your AT Protocol identity, account, records, or media stored on a Personal Data Server (PDS). This includes a Coves-hosted PDS. PDS deletion is a separate process. Other services may also hold copies of content that was previously public; Coves cannot directly delete copies controlled by those services.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data associated with your account
  • Delete: Request deletion of your Coves AppView account and associated data at Delete Account, subject to the limited retention described above
  • Control: Manage your data directly on your PDS
  • Portability: Your data lives on your PDS and can be moved to another atProto service

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@coves.social.

8. Age Requirement

Coves is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please do not use this service.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a user under 18, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on this page
  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top
  • Notifying you through the app when appropriate

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Future Features

We may introduce analytics, crash reporting, or other features in the future to improve the service. If we do, we will:

  • Update this Privacy Policy before implementing such features
  • Use privacy-respecting options where possible
  • Be transparent about what data is collected and why
